# Ramadan Dream Launcher — Blender scene assembly

The validated CC0 source models are assembled into one real 3D scene with Blender.
The scene retains the source GLB meshes and PBR textures, applies an emerald
tint to selected mosque stone/roof materials, and adds real mesh/curve/text
geometry for the gold rope, seven emerald silk flags with raised Arabic-style
lettering, crescent, star, lantern flames, and courtyard.

The Blender export includes looping animation channels for flag sway, flame
flicker, warm lantern light intensity, and subtle kitten breathing. The final
`ramadan_dream_scene.glb` is the single runtime scene consumed by the Android
Filament renderer.

The source model license and original URLs remain in `ASSET_MANIFEST.json`.
The tree is an artistic broad-canopy interpretation inspired by the Jordanian
Al-Buqayawiyya tradition; it is not claimed to be an exact botanical model.
